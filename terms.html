Terms &
Policies

Please read our terms and policies carefully before using our services. By booking or visiting Escape House Abuja you agree to these terms.

01. Booking & Reservation Policy

All bookings at Escape House Abuja — including Spa, Hotel Apartments, Restaurant and Salon — are subject to availability and must be confirmed by our team before they are considered valid.

Reservations can be made through our website booking form, WhatsApp or by visiting us in person. A booking is only confirmed once you receive a confirmation message from our team.

We reserve the right to decline any booking at our discretion.

02. Cancellation & Refund Policy

We understand that plans change. Our cancellation policy is as follows:

  • Spa & Salon: Cancellations made at least 24 hours before your appointment will receive a full refund or rescheduling at no charge. Cancellations within 24 hours may be subject to a cancellation fee.
  • Hotel Apartments: Cancellations made at least 48 hours before check-in will receive a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours of check-in may forfeit the first night's payment.
  • Restaurant: For general dining, no cancellation fee applies. For private dining reservations, please notify us at least 24 hours in advance.
  • Coded Escape House: All Coded reservations require at least 48 hours notice for cancellation. Late cancellations may be subject to a fee at our discretion.

Refunds where applicable will be processed within 3-5 business days via the original payment method.

03. Payment Terms

We accept the following payment methods at all Escape House locations:

  • Cash (Nigerian Naira)
  • Bank Transfer
  • Debit & Credit Cards
  • Mobile Banking (USSD)

All prices are displayed in Nigerian Naira (₦) and are inclusive of applicable taxes. Prices are subject to change without prior notice.

For hotel apartment bookings, a deposit may be required to secure your reservation. Our team will advise you of the deposit amount at the time of booking.

04. House Rules

To ensure a premium experience for all our guests, we ask that all visitors observe the following house rules:

  • Please respect the privacy and comfort of other guests at all times.
  • Noise levels must be kept to a minimum in all common areas and accommodation.
  • Smoking is strictly prohibited in all indoor areas.
  • Pets are not permitted on the premises.
  • Smart casual attire is required in the restaurant and all common areas.
  • Escape House reserves the right to refuse service to any guest who behaves in a disrespectful or disruptive manner.
  • Any damage caused to property will be charged to the responsible party.

05. Privacy Policy

Escape House Abuja is committed to protecting your personal information. When you make a booking through our website, we collect your name, phone number, email address and booking details.

This information is used solely to process your booking, confirm your reservation and communicate with you regarding your visit. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

Your booking data is stored securely and you may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us directly.

06. Coded Escape House Policy

Coded Escape House operates under additional terms due to its exclusive nature:

  • Access is strictly by reservation only. Walk-ins are not accepted.
  • All guests are required to provide valid identification upon arrival.
  • Photography and recording within Coded Escape House is strictly prohibited without prior written consent.
  • Guest information and visits are treated with the strictest confidentiality.
  • Coded Escape House reserves the right to refuse entry at its sole discretion.
